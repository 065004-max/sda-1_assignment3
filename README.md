## Business Insights
1. Stock Price Comparison: The dashboard compares the average stock prices of RELIANCE, TCS and INFY. This helps identify differences in their recorded price levels and understand how they compare with one another.

2. Stock Price Trends: The line chart shows price movements over time for all three companies. It helps observe fluctuations, identify periods of price increases or decreases and compare their movements.

3. Trading Volume Analysis: The trading volume chart compares the average trading volumes of the three companies. It provides an overview of their recorded trading activity and helps identify differences in average volumes.

4. Interactive Analysis: The stock selector and time filter allow users to focus on particular stocks and time periods. Periodic dashboard refresh also helps display newly received data without repeatedly reloading the dashboard manually.

5. Overall Observation: The dashboard brings stock prices, price trends and trading volumes together in one place. It makes it easier to compare companies and monitor changes in the incoming data.
